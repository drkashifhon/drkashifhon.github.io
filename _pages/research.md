My research focuses on **artificial intelligence for healthcare**, with particular emphasis on physiological time-series, continuous glucose monitoring, wearable sensing, and speech-based neurological assessment. I develop machine-learning methods for extracting clinically meaningful information from complex physiological and behavioral data.

## Diabetes & Continuous Glucose Monitoring

My research investigates data-driven methods for understanding glucose dynamics and detecting clinically relevant events from continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) and related physiological data.

Current topics include:

- Meal and eating-event detection from CGM
- Integration of CGM and insulin information
- Glucose-level forecasting
- Long-window physiological time-series modeling
- Event-level detection and post-processing
- False-positive-aware model calibration

## Wearable Sensing & Eating Behavior

I work on machine-learning approaches for recognizing eating behavior from wearable sensors, particularly wrist-worn inertial measurement units (IMUs).

This research includes:

- Wrist-motion analysis
- Eating-event detection
- Temporal deep learning
- Subject-independent evaluation
- Episode-level event detection
- Multimodal wearable sensing

## Speech-Based Neurological Assessment

Another part of my research explores speech as a digital biomarker for neurological assessment.

My work investigates:

- Self-supervised speech representations
- Automatic speech recognition
- Parameter-efficient fine-tuning
- Prompt-aligned speech analysis
- Acoustic and linguistic information fusion
- Subject-level neurological classification

## Multimodal AI for Healthcare

I am broadly interested in integrating heterogeneous sources of physiological and behavioral information to develop robust AI systems for personalized health monitoring.

My research combines:

- Physiological signals
- Wearable sensor data
- Speech
- Clinical and contextual information
- Deep learning
- Temporal modeling
- Multimodal machine learning

## Research Methodology

Across these areas, I am particularly interested in:

- Robust subject-independent validation
- Physiologically meaningful model design
- Temporal and sequential learning
- Event detection from continuous signals
- Multimodal data fusion
- Clinically interpretable evaluation
- Generalization across individuals and datasets
