My research projects focus on artificial intelligence for healthcare, physiological time-series analysis, wearable sensing, continuous glucose monitoring, and speech-based neurological assessment.

## Meal Episode Detection from CGM and Insulin Data

Development of deep learning methods for detecting meal-related episodes from continuous glucose monitoring and insulin data.

The work investigates:

- Long-window physiological time-series modeling
- CGM and insulin integration
- BiLSTM-based sequence learning
- Event-level post-processing
- False-positive-aware threshold calibration
- Subject-independent evaluation

This project aims to identify meal-related physiological responses from continuously collected diabetes data without relying entirely on manual meal annotations.

---

## Wearable Eating-Event Detection

Development of machine-learning approaches for detecting eating episodes from wrist-worn inertial sensors.

Research topics include:

- Accelerometer-based eating detection
- Temporal deep learning
- CNN and recurrent neural networks
- Episode-level event detection
- Subject-independent validation
- Real-world wearable sensing

The work investigates how wrist-motion patterns can be transformed into reliable estimates of eating behavior in free-living conditions.

## Speech-Based Parkinson's Assessment

Development of AI methods for neurological assessment from speech.

The project combines:

- Self-supervised speech representations
- WavLM
- Parameter-efficient fine-tuning
- Automatic speech recognition with Whisper
- Prompt-aligned ASR error analysis
- Acoustic and linguistic feature fusion

The goal is to investigate speech-based digital biomarkers for robust subject-level neurological assessment.

---

## Glucose Level Prediction

Development of temporal deep-learning models for predicting future glucose levels from continuous glucose monitoring data.

Research areas include:

- LSTM-based forecasting
- Physiological time-series modeling
- Glucose dynamics
- Prediction accuracy and temporal evaluation
- Personalized diabetes monitoring

This work contributed to my research on deep-learning approaches for glucose prediction and physiological forecasting.
